Where these numbers come from

Money: Federal Election Commission summary file for the 2025–26 cycle (weball26), principal campaign committee only, through each member's latest report. "PACs" means contributions from PACs and other political committees. Leadership PACs and outside spending are not included. Members not raising money this cycle show no totals.

Votes: Every recorded roll call of the 119th Congress through , via Voteview (UCLA), which compiles the House Clerk and Senate records. "Missed" counts roll calls where the member did not vote. "With party" is the share of yes/no votes cast on the same side as the majority of the member's party; independents are compared with the Democratic caucus they join.

Key votes: Twenty roll calls chosen because they bear on this project's three questions: veterans and military leadership, the federal workforce, and civil rights and voting, plus the year's largest budget votes. Each links to the official House Clerk or Senate record. The selection is ours; check the full record for everything else.

Members: congress-legislators public dataset. Snapshot built . These are measures, not verdicts. Read the records, then decide for yourself.
